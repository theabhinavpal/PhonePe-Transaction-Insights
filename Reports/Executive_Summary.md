# Executive Summary

**PhonePe Transaction Insights** analyses 5.47 billion digital-payment
transactions worth **Rs 4.52 lakh crore** across **36 states/UTs**,
**6 years (2018-2023)** and **4 quarters**.

## Headline results

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total transaction value | Rs 4.52 lakh crore |
| Total transaction volume | 5.47 billion |
| Average ticket size | Rs 827 |
| Latest quarter (Q4 2023) value | Rs 29,606 Cr |
| Value CAGR (2018-2023) | 18.3% |
| Latest full-year YoY growth | 12.04% |
| Registered users (latest quarter) | 7.15 Cr |
| App opens (latest quarter) | 54.75 Cr |
| Insurance premium (since 2020) | Rs 882 Cr |

## What the data says

1. **Growth is strong but maturing.** Value compounded at **18.3%** a year
   from 2018 to 2023, but the annual growth rate has cooled to
   **12.04%** in the latest year - the classic S-curve of a maturing market.
2. **The South leads the country**, contributing **32.45%** of all
   value, ahead of North (20.92%) and
   West (16.13%).
3. **Value is highly concentrated.** The top state (Maharashtra) alone holds
   **10.05%** of value and the top 5 states hold
   **39.8%**. At district level the top 20% of districts capture
   **63.92%** of value - a textbook Pareto distribution.
4. **The payment mix is shifting.** Merchant payments grew from
   **14.89% to 24.84%** of value between 2018 and
   2023, while Recharge & bill payments fell from
   **15.48% to 9.71%** - evidence of the shift from
   utility use-cases to everyday retail spending.
5. **Seasonality is real and monetisable.** Q4 (the festival quarter) runs on
   average **40.25%** above Q1.

## Recommendations

- **Defend the core, expand the frontier:** protect share in the top-5 states while
  running Tier-2/Tier-3 acquisition where district-level growth is fastest.
- **Lean into merchant payments:** the fastest-growing category by share; invest in
  QR onboarding and merchant lending hooks.
- **Time campaigns to the festival quarter** to capture the 40.25% seasonal lift.
- **Grow insurance in the long tail:** premium is concentrated in Maharashtra
  and Karnataka; large user bases elsewhere are under-penetrated.
